Terms of Service

Last updated October 22, 2026

The short version
  • The homepage concept we send you is free. You never owe anything for it.
  • We build your full site for free. You pay the flat $1,000 only if you approve it.
  • Once you pay, the site, its files and its design are yours.
  • The Care Plan is optional, month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

Who we are

BringsUp ("we", "us") designs and builds websites for local businesses. These terms cover bringsup.com, the free concepts we send, and any website we build for you. By submitting one of our forms, signing, or paying, you agree to them.

Free concepts

We sometimes design a sample homepage for a business before being asked, using information that is already public, like your current website and reviews. Concepts are ideas, not live websites. They aren't published anywhere except a private link we send you, and they don't mean you're affiliated with us or have agreed to anything.

Don't want one? Reply to our email or write to hello@bringsup.com and we'll delete it and won't contact you again.

Building your website

When you fill in our start form and sign, you're asking us to build your full website. We aim to send a private preview within about two weeks. Your project includes two rounds of changes, requested by form or email. Extra rounds beyond that, or brand-new pages that weren't in scope, may be quoted separately, and we'll always tell you the price before doing the work.

Approval and payment

You pay nothing up front. If you approve your finished site, you pay a flat fee of $1,000 (US dollars) by card through Stripe when you approve it. If you decide not to approve it, you can walk away and you owe nothing. We don't publish an unapproved site.

Because you see and approve the finished work before paying, payments are generally non-refundable. If something we built doesn't work as shown in the approved preview, tell us within 30 days of launch and we'll fix it at no charge.

Who owns what

Once you've paid, you own the finished website, its design and its files, and we'll hand over everything if you ask. Your domain always stays in your name. We may show your site in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

You keep ownership of everything you send us, like your logo, photos and text. You give us permission to use it to build and run your site, and you confirm you have the right to share it. Stock photos we add are licensed for use on your website.

The Care Plan

The Care Plan is optional. It covers hosting, security updates and small edits (things like new hours, specials, photos or text changes) for a monthly fee shown on our pricing section, billed by Stripe. It renews monthly until you cancel. You can cancel anytime by emailing us, and it stops at the end of that month. If you leave, we help move your site wherever you'd like.

Without the Care Plan, you're responsible for hosting your site, and we'll send you the files and setup steps.

Your domain and email

Connecting your domain to your new site may mean changing settings at your domain provider. We send step-by-step instructions and take care to leave your email settings alone, but you're responsible for your domain account, and we aren't liable for issues caused by your provider or by changes we didn't make.

You agree that signing our forms electronically, by drawing your signature or ticking a box to agree, is legally binding, the same as signing on paper, and that we can send agreements, receipts and notices to you by email.

What we're not responsible for

We work hard to build sites that bring in customers, but we can't promise specific results, rankings or sales. Our sites are provided as approved, and to the fullest extent the law allows, our total responsibility for any claim related to our work is limited to the amount you paid us in the 12 months before the claim. We're not liable for indirect losses like lost profits.

Ending things
