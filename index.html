FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Something else? Email hello@bringsup.com and you'll hear back from a person.

Is the concept really free?

Yes. We make your homepage concept before we ever talk money. If it's not for you, that's the end of it. No follow-up calls, no invoice.

How did you find my business?

Your reviews. We look for family-owned businesses with a great reputation on Google and a website that hasn't caught up yet. Everything in your concept came from what you've already published.

Why make a concept before I've even asked?

Because "we'll make you a great website" is easy to say. Showing you is better. If you like what you see, we both save time.

Do I have to change my domain or email?

No. We launch the new site on the domain you already have, and your email stays exactly as it is.

What do you need from me?

About 20 minutes on a call, plus any photos you like. We pull your hours, menu or services and reviews from what's already online.

What if I need more than 5 pages, or online ordering?

Tell us on the call. Order, booking and quote buttons are part of the $1,000. If you need something bigger, we'll give you a flat price before any work starts.

Who owns the website?

You do. The design, the files and the domain are yours, whether or not you keep the Care Plan.

Can I update it myself?

Yes, you get a simple editor. Or send us the change and we'll handle it on the Care Plan.

What if I already have a web person?

Great. Share the concept with them. We're happy to hand over the design, or to work alongside them.
